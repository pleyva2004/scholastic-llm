\begin{multicols}{2}

\section*{Motivation}
Instruction-tuned LLMs default to a flat, encyclopedic register.
Specialized historical writing --- the Summa's \emph{quaestio} form,
Augustine's autobiographical address, the Catechism's numbered paragraph
citations --- has a distinct lexical and structural fingerprint that is
absent from base-model output. Can a 7B open-weights model be moved into
that register \emph{cheaply}, on a laptop, using only public-domain or
fair-use sources?

\section*{Pipeline}
\begin{center}
\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{\input{figures/pipeline}}
\end{center}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Scrape CCC (vatican.va), Summa, Augustine (newadvent.org) with
        rate-limiting and on-disk cache.
  \item Clean into 342 structured JSONL chunks.
  \item Claude Sonnet 4.6 as \emph{teacher}: per chunk, 2--3
        (question, scholastic-answer) pairs with explicit register and
        CCC-citation instructions. 93 pairs, $\approx\$0.50$.
  \item Supervised fine-tune Qwen 2.5\,7B-Instruct (MLX Q8) with LoRA.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Training setup}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Base \texttt{Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct}, MLX 8-bit quantization
  \item LoRA on top 16 of 28 transformer layers
  \item AdamW, LR $10^{-5}$, batch 1, max seq 2048
  \item 200 iters, 83 train + 10 valid; wall time $\approx$\,7~min
  \item Hardware Apple M4 Pro, 48\,GB unified memory
  \item Peak memory 12.7\,GB; trainable params 2.6M / 7.6B (0.034\,\%)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Held-out evaluation}
10 philosophical prompts not seen during training: nature of evil,
faith and reason, grace and free will, soul/body, suffering, knowing
God, neighbor-love, natural law, divine foreknowledge, meaning of life.
Rubric: 4 dimensions, each 0--3 per prompt, summed across 10 prompts
(per-dimension max 30, total max 120).

\section*{Results}
\begin{center}
\resizebox{\linewidth}{!}{\input{figures/rubric-bars}}
\end{center}

\noindent Per-dimension deltas (BASE $\to$ SFT, max 30): scholastic
register \textbf{3$\to$20} (+17), CCC grounding \textbf{0$\to$19} (+19),
structure \textbf{16$\to$26} (+10), Augustinian voice 0$\to$3.

\section*{Qualitative example}
\noindent\textbf{Q.} \emph{How do you reconcile divine foreknowledge
with free will?}

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{BASE:} ``The reconciliation of divine
foreknowledge with free will is a classic philosophical and theological
problem that has been debated for centuries\dots''

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{SFT:} ``This is perhaps the most ancient and
most pressing question in all of metaphysics, and it is one that the
Angelic Doctor addresses with great precision. \textbf{Objection~1.} It
would seem that divine foreknowledge destroys free will, for if God
knows\dots''

\section*{Phase 2: voice rebalancing + DPO}
Scaled to 377 pairs with per-source register hints (Aquinas for
\emph{Summa}/CCC, Augustinian for \emph{Confessions}/\emph{City of
God}), 800 SFT iters. Best checkpoint \textbf{iter 400}.
\textbf{Augustinian gap closed} (\texttt{aug} $3\to7\text{--}13$); under
a balanced rubric that credits register fluency
($\max(\textsc{reg},\textsc{aug})$), Phase~2 reaches \textbf{68/90 vs
Phase~1's 66/90}.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Negative result.} A DPO chain
(\emph{chosen}~$=$~SFT output, \emph{rejected}~$=$~base output) was
inert: val loss \textbf{0.000} from iter~1, accuracy 1.000, margin
35.4~nats. Within-model preference data saturates the policy at
initialization. Useful DPO needs pairs the policy hasn't already pre-learned to favor or disfavor.

\section*{Takeaway}
Small open-weights models move into a niche historical register cheaply
when capacity (7B not 1.5B) and quantization (Q8 not Q4) are right. The
bottleneck is the \emph{teacher signal}, not the optimization recipe.
