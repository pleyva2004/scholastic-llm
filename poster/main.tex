\section*{Motivation}
Instruction-tuned LLMs default to a flat, encyclopedic register.
Specialized historical writing --- the Summa's \emph{quaestio} form,
Augustine's autobiographical address, the Catechism's numbered paragraph
citations --- has a distinct lexical and structural fingerprint that is
absent from base-model output. Can a 7B open-weights model be moved into
that register \emph{cheaply}, on a laptop, using only public-domain or
fair-use sources?

\section*{Pipeline}
\begin{center}
\resizebox{0.7\linewidth}{!}{\input{figures/pipeline}}
\end{center}
\vspace{-0.4em}
\noindent Scrape CCC + Summa + Augustine; clean to 342 JSONL chunks;
use Claude Sonnet 4.6 as \emph{teacher} to write 93 scholastic Q\&A
pairs ($\approx\$0.50$); SFT with LoRA.

\section*{Training setup}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Base \texttt{Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct}, MLX 8-bit quantization
  \item LoRA on top 16 of 28 transformer layers
  \item AdamW, LR $10^{-5}$, batch 1, max seq 2048
  \item 200 iters, 83 train + 10 valid; wall time $\approx$\,7~min
  \item Hardware Apple M4 Pro, 48\,GB unified memory
  \item Peak memory 12.7\,GB; trainable params 2.6M / 7.6B (0.034\,\%)
\end{itemize}

\section*{Results}
\begin{center}
\resizebox{0.95\linewidth}{!}{\input{figures/rubric-bars}}
\end{center}
\vspace{-0.6em}

\noindent Per-dimension deltas (BASE $\to$ SFT, max 30) across 10
held-out philosophical prompts not seen in training: scholastic
register \textbf{3$\to$20} (+17), CCC grounding \textbf{0$\to$19} (+19),
structure \textbf{16$\to$26} (+10), Augustinian voice 0$\to$3.

\section*{Qualitative example}
\noindent\textbf{Q.} \emph{How do you reconcile divine foreknowledge
with free will?}

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{BASE:} ``The reconciliation of divine
foreknowledge with free will is a classic philosophical and theological
problem\dots''

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{SFT:} ``\dots the Angelic Doctor addresses
[this] with great precision. \textbf{Objection~1.} It would seem that
divine foreknowledge destroys free will, for if God knows\dots''

\section*{Phase 2 \& DPO negative result}
Scaled to 377 pairs with per-source register hints (Aquinas for
\emph{Summa}/CCC, Augustinian for \emph{Confessions}/\emph{City of
God}), 800 SFT iters; best checkpoint \textbf{iter 400}.
\textbf{Augustinian gap closed} (\texttt{aug}: $3 \to 7$--$13$); under a
balanced rubric ($\max(\textsc{reg},\textsc{aug}) + \textsc{ccc} +
\textsc{str}$), Phase~2 reaches \textbf{68/90 vs Phase~1's 66/90}. A
DPO chain (chosen $=$ SFT output, rejected $=$ base) was \emph{inert}:
val loss 0.000 from iter~1, accuracy 1.000, margin 35.4~nats.
Within-model preference data saturates the policy at initialization.

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{Takeaway.} Small open-weights models move into a
niche historical register cheaply when capacity (7B not 1.5B) and
quantization (Q8 not Q4) are right. The bottleneck is the \emph{teacher
signal}, not the optimization recipe.
